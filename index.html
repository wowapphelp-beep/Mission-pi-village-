What is Mission Pi Village?

Mission Pi Village is a community initiative focused on education, digital awareness, blockchain knowledge, local entrepreneurship and ecosystem development.

Our Mission

Our journey begins with education and community building and gradually moves toward local digital and economic utility.

🎓 Education

Learn blockchain, wallets, digital security, Pi Network and Web3 concepts.

👑 Village Leader

Build a local team and coordinate Mission Pi Village activities.

🏪 Merchants

Connect local shops, businesses and service providers with the community.

🔄 Utility

Encourage real goods, services and useful digital ecosystem activities.

From Village to Global Network 🌍

One village can start a movement. Connected villages can build a network.
